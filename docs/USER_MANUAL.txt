# PART 1: Understanding the COEPD Quality-Gated Placement System

### Why is this platform different from open job boards?
On regular job boards (like LinkedIn, Indeed, or Naukri), anyone can apply to any job post. Hiring managers often receive 500+ resumes for a single opening, of which 90% are unqualified, leading to frustration, ghosting, and long hiring delays.

**COEPD HRAnalyst works on a Strict Quality Gate:**
1. **Guaranteed Verification:** Students cannot face an external employer until an internal Placement HR officer has reviewed their profile, verified their credentials, and checked their resume against job-specific criteria.
2. **Mandatory Mock Interview Clearance:** Even if a student has an impressive CV, they must pass an official Mock Interview with an internal expert before their profile can be shared with an employer.
3. **Curated Batches, Not Spam:** Employers receive clean, curated batches of 3 to 10 top-matched, pre-tested candidates with frozen profile snapshots.
4. **Complete Transparency:** Students see their exact position in the recruitment pipeline at all times—no silent rejections or black-hole applications.

# PART 2: Student User Handbook (Candidate Guide)

Welcome, Candidate! This section guides you from your first login all the way to accepting your dream job offer.

---

### 1. Registration, Account Setup & First Login
1. Open the portal URL in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari recommended).
2. Click **"Register as Candidate"** or go to the `/register` page.
3. Fill in your basic details:
   - **First & Last Name:** Enter your official legal name as it appears on certificates.
   - **Email Address:** Use your active email; all job alerts and interview links are sent here.
   - **Phone Number:** 10-digit mobile number for WhatsApp and SMS alerts.
   - **Password:** At least 8 characters with numbers and letters.
   - **Select Role:** Choose **Student**.
4. Click **"Create Account"**.
5. **Account Approval Notice:** Your account will initially show as `Pending Approval`. An administrator will verify your institute enrollment within 24 hours. Once approved, you will receive an onboarding email and can log in immediately.

---

### 2. Building an Outstanding Profile (Field-by-Field)
Navigate to **Profile** (`/student/profile`) from the left navigation bar.

> **Why Profile Completion Matters:**  
> Employers and Placement HR filter candidates by profile completion. **You must reach at least 80% completion** to unlock applications for top-tier hiring drives.

#### Personal & Contact Information
- **Profile Photo / Avatar:** Upload a clear, professional headshot with a neutral background.
- **City, State, Country:** Your current location of residence.
- **LinkedIn Profile URL:** Add your complete LinkedIn URL (e.g., `https://linkedin.com/in/yourname`).
- **GitHub / Portfolio URL:** Optional, but highly recommended for technical roles or Business Analyst case studies.

#### Professional Summary & Headline
- **Headline:** A concise professional title (e.g., *"Certified Business Analyst | Agile, JIRA & User Stories Specialist"*).
- **Summary:** A 3–5 sentence overview highlighting your core strengths, project work at COEPD, and career goals.

#### Work History & Experience
- **Total Experience:** Enter in total months (e.g., `24` for 2 years). If you are a fresher or career transitioner, enter `0`.
- **Current Designation & Company:** Fill in if currently employed; otherwise leave blank or enter *"Candidate / Trainee"*.
- **Current CTC & Expected CTC:** Enter annual figures in Indian Rupees (e.g., `5,00,000` for 5 LPA).
- **Notice Period:** Number of days required before you can join a new company (e.g., `0` for Immediate Joiner, `15`, `30`, or `60` days).

#### Role & Location Preferences
- **Preferred Role:** Select your target domain:
  - `BA` — Business Analyst
  - `PO` — Product Owner
  - `PM` — Project Manager
- **Preferred Location Type:** Choose `Domestic` (within India), `International`, or `Both`.
- **Preferred Cities:** Multi-select cities (e.g., Hyderabad, Pune, Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Remote).
- **Willing to Relocate:** Toggle `Yes` if you are open to moving for the right opportunity.

Click **"Save Profile Changes"**. A success banner will confirm your update, and your completion meter will increase.

### 3. Uploading and Managing Your Resumes (CVs)
Navigate to **My Resumes** (`/student/cv`).

1. **Preparing Your CV File:**
   - Supported formats: **PDF** (recommended) or **DOCX**.
   - Maximum file size: **5 MB**.
   - Ensure clean formatting: Use standard section headings (*Summary*, *Skills*, *Experience*, *Projects*, *Education*).
2. **Uploading:**
   - Drag and drop your file into the upload box or click **"Browse Files"**.
   - The platform automatically scans and processes your resume using an intelligent parser to extract your skills and experience.
3. **Setting a Primary Resume:**
   - You can upload multiple resumes (e.g., one tailored for Healthcare BA, another for Banking/FinTech BA).
   - Click **"Make Primary"** on your preferred resume. This resume will be auto-attached whenever you apply for jobs with one click.
4. **Previewing & Downloading:** Click the eye icon to view your resume in the built-in reader or the download icon to save a copy.

### 4. Exploring the Job Feed & Understanding Match Scores
Navigate to **Job Feed** (`/student/jobs`).

#### Understanding Your AI Match Score
Next to every job card, you will see a colored badge displaying your **Match Score**:
- 🟢 **80% - 100% (High Match):** Your uploaded resume and profile closely match the job’s must-have tools, skills, and experience. **Apply immediately!**
- 🟡 **60% - 79% (Moderate Match):** You meet the core requirements but may lack a secondary tool or preference. Still a good opportunity.
- ⚪ **Below 60% (Low Match):** Major skill or experience gap. You can still apply, but review the missing skills highlighted on the card.

#### Filtering Jobs
Use the top filter bar to narrow down opportunities:
- **Workplace Mode:** Filter by `Onsite`, `Hybrid`, or `Remote`.
- **Experience Bracket:** Freshers (0–1 yr), Mid-level (2–4 yrs), Senior (5+ yrs).
- **Location:** Filter by specific cities.
- **Salary Range:** Filter by minimum CTC.

2. Review the full information:
   - Hiring Company Name & Logo
   - Location & Workplace Type

   - Annual Salary Range

   - The dropdown will default to your **Primary Resume**. You can select a different uploaded resume if preferred.
   - Click the green **"Apply Now"** button.
   - A confirmation dialog will appear. Click **"Confirm Application"**.

### 6. Tracking Your Application Journey (Status Definitions)
Navigate to **Applications** (`/student/applications`).

Click on any application to open its detailed **Application Tracker** (`/student/applications/[id]`). You will see an interactive timeline tracking your application step-by-step:

| Status Name | What It Means | What You Should Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Applied** | Your application has been logged into the system. | Await review by Placement HR. |
| **Verification Pending** | A Placement HR officer is reviewing your CV and credentials. | Keep your phone and email active. |
| **Update Requested** | Placement HR requested updates to your profile or CV. | Click the update link, follow HR's notes, revise your CV, and re-submit. |
| **Verified** | Internal verification cleared! You passed the initial quality gate. | Proceed to book your internal Mock Interview. |
| **Mock Pending / Scheduled** | Mock interview appointment is booked. | Review core BA concepts and prepare your case studies. |
| **Submitted to Client** | Your verified profile has been submitted in a curated batch to the employer. | The employer is currently evaluating your profile. |
| **Shortlisted** | The employer liked your profile and selected you for an interview! | Await your formal interview calendar invite. |
| **Interview Scheduled** | An external interview round has been fixed with the employer. | Check meeting link/address and attend on time. |
| **Offer Received** | Congratulations! The employer has extended an official offer letter. | Review CTC and terms, and make your decision. |
| **Placed** | You accepted the offer and are officially placed! | Celebrate your achievement! |
| **Rejected** | Application was not selected at this stage. | Review the feedback reason provided and apply for other jobs. |

#### Voluntary Withdrawal
If you are no longer interested in a position or have accepted another offer:
1. Open the application details page.
2. Click **"Withdraw Application"**.
3. Select or type your reason (e.g., *"Accepted another offer"*, *"Location constraint"*).
4. Click **"Confirm Withdrawal"**. This frees up the opening for your fellow students.

### 7. The Mandatory Mock Interview (Booking, Preparing & Scorecards)
Navigate to **Mock Interviews** (`/student/mocks`).

> **The Golden Rule:** You cannot be submitted to any client company without first clearing an internal Mock Interview with a recommendation of `Ready`.

#### Booking a Mock Slot
1. When your application status reaches **Verified**, the mock interview booking section unlocks.
2. Choose from available dates and time slots published by the training faculty.
3. Select the mode: `Online` (Google Meet/Zoom) or `Offline` (Institute Campus).
4. Click **"Confirm Booking"**. You will receive an immediate calendar confirmation.

#### Attending the Mock
1. Log in 10 minutes prior to your scheduled time.
2. Click the **"Join Meeting"** button directly from your Mock dashboard.
3. Dress professionally and have your projects, BA artifacts (BRD, FRD, User Stories, Use Cases), and case studies ready.

#### Reviewing Your Scorecard
Within 24 hours of completing the mock, your evaluator posts an official **Mock Scorecard**:
- **Communication Score (0–100):** Articulation, confidence, and clarity.
- **Technical & Analytical Score (0–100):** Knowledge of BA tools, SQL, Agile/Scrum, and modeling.
- **Domain Score (0–100):** Understanding of business processes and real-world scenarios.
- **Official Recommendation:**
  - 🟢 **Ready:** You cleared the quality gate! Your profile is immediately unlocked for client submissions.
  - 🟡 **Ready with Minor Improvements:** Cleared, but read the specific notes for improvement before facing clients.
  - 🔴 **Needs Another Mock:** Not cleared yet. Review the evaluator's feedback, practice the suggested areas, and book another mock slot.

### 11. Student FAQs & Troubleshooting

**Q: Why is my account still "Pending Approval"?**  
A: New student accounts are verified against student enrollment records to ensure platform authenticity. If your account remains pending after 24 hours, contact your institute student coordinator.

**Q: Why is my Match Score low for a job I want?**  
A: The match score calculates overlap between the words in your CV and the job's required checklist. Review the checklist skills and ensure that your resume accurately mentions all tools and methodologies you know.

**Q: Can I edit my application after submitting?**  
A: No, but you can withdraw and re-apply with a revised resume as long as the job deadline has not passed.

**Q: What if I have a network issue during an online interview?**  
A: Immediately reach out to your assigned Placement HR contact via phone or the notification desk so they can coordinate with the employer for a quick reconnect or reschedule.

### 2. Posting Jobs (Internal Drives & Client Requisitions)
Navigate to **Jobs** (`/placement-hr/jobs`).

Placement HRs post jobs for internal placement drives or on behalf of corporate partners who submit requirements via email/call:
1. Click the blue **"+ Post New Job"** button (`/placement-hr/jobs/new`).
2. Fill out the comprehensive job creation form:
   - **Job Title:** Clear, standardized title (e.g., *Junior Business Analyst - FinTech*).
   - **Company:** Select from the dropdown of approved corporate partners.
   - **Role Category:** Select role type (`BA`, `PO`, `PM`, `DA`, `QA`, `SD`).
   - **Workplace Mode:** Select `Onsite`, `Hybrid`, or `Remote`.
   - **Location:** Enter city/country.
   - **Experience Requirement:** Minimum and Maximum years (e.g., `0` to `2` years).
   - **Salary Range:** Minimum and Maximum Annual CTC in INR (e.g., `4,50,000` to `6,50,000`).
   - **Openings:** Number of available vacancies.
   - **Application Deadline:** Date and time after which no new applications will be accepted.
   - **Job Description (JD):** Paste the complete JD text.
   - **Assigned Placement HR:** Assign yourself or a team colleague responsible for this drive.
3. Click **"Save Job as Draft"**. The job is created in `pending_checklist` status.

### 3. Generating and Refining AI CV Checklists
Navigate to the Job Detail page (`/placement-hr/jobs/[id]`).

> **Why Checklists are Critical:**  
> The Checklist is the standard benchmark used by the automated scoring engine and human verifiers. It ensures that every candidate is evaluated against objective criteria.

1. On the Job Detail page, locate the **CV Checklist** card.
2. Click **"Generate Checklist using AI"**.
3. In seconds, the platform uses AI to analyze the JD and extract:
   - **Must-Have Skills:** Non-negotiable core skills (e.g., *User Stories*, *BRD/FRD Documentation*, *Requirement Gathering*, *SQL Basics*).
   - **Good-to-Have Skills:** Secondary advantages (e.g., *JIRA*, *Confluence*, *Balsamiq*, *Wireframing*).
   - **Tools & Methodologies:** Specific tools (e.g., *Agile/Scrum*, *Draw.io*, *Excel Advanced*).
   - **Domain & Experience:** Target industry (e.g., *Banking*, *Healthcare*, *E-Commerce*) and minimum experience.
4. **Refining the Checklist:**
   - Click the pencil icon to add or remove skills.
   - Reorder items to reflect priority.
5. **Approve & Publish:**
   - Click **"Approve Checklist"**.
   - Click **"Publish Job"**.
   - The job immediately moves to `published` status and is broadcast to all eligible students' job feeds with real-time notifications.

### 4. Verifying Student Applications (The Verification Workspace)
Navigate to **Applications** (`/placement-hr/applications`).

#### Step 1: Claiming an Application
To prevent duplicate reviews among team members:
1. Browse the **Unassigned Applications** queue.
2. Click **"Claim Application"**. The application is now exclusively assigned to you.

#### Step 2: Conducting the Verification (`/placement-hr/applications/[id]`)
Opening the application displays a side-by-side workspace:
- **Left Panel:** Student profile summary, parsed skills, work history, and interactive CV reader.
- **Right Panel:** Verification Checklist and Decision Panel.

1. **Audit Checklist Criteria (Check each box as verified):**
   - [x] **Must-Have Skills Verified:** Confirm the student genuinely possesses the required core skills.
   - [x] **CV Format & Authenticity Verified:** Ensure the CV is clear, well-structured, and error-free.
   - [x] **Experience Verified:** Confirm experience claims match enrollment records.
   - [x] **Domain Alignment Verified:** Confirm the candidate's background matches the job domain.
2. **Reviewing and Adjusting Scores:**
   - The system displays the automated Match Score and ATS Score.
   - If the student has strong transferable skills not captured by the automated parser, you can manually adjust the **Verified Match Score**.
3. **Making the Final Verification Decision:**
   - **VERIFY:** Click **"Mark as Verified"**. The student is marked verified and receives an in-app and email alert to book their Mock Interview.
   - **REJECT:** Click **"Reject Application"**. You must select or type a clear rejection reason (e.g., *"Requires minimum 2 years banking experience"*). The student receives a constructive rejection notice.
   - **REQUEST UPDATE:** Click **"Request Revisions"**. Enter specific instructions (e.g., *"Please add your SQL academic project details to section 3 and re-upload your CV"*). The student is notified to update their application.

### 5. Managing Mock Interviews & Submitting Scorecards
Navigate to **Mock Interviews** (`/placement-hr/mocks`).

#### Managing Slot Availability
1. Click **"Manage Availability Slots"**.
2. Add available evaluation slots: Date, start time, end time, evaluator name, and mode (`Online`/`Offline`).
3. These slots instantly appear on verified students' booking calendars.

#### Conducting Mocks & Recording Scorecards (`/placement-hr/mocks/[id]`)
1. Click **"Start Session"** on the scheduled mock card.
2. Interview the candidate on their BA competencies, case-study problem solving, and communication.
3. Open the **Scorecard Form**:
   - **Communication Score (0–100):** English fluency, professional presentation, clarity of thought.
   - **Technical & Analytical Score (0–100):** Understanding of requirements, user stories, SQL, and UML diagrams.
   - **Domain Knowledge (0–100):** Process flows and business acumen.
4. **Enter Feedback Comments:**
   - **Strengths:** Highlight where the candidate excelled.
   - **Areas for Improvement:** Actionable points for the student to practice.
5. **Select Recommendation:**
   - `ready` ➔ **PASS:** Clears the Quality Gate. Unlocks the student for client submission batches.
   - `ready_with_minor_improvement` ➔ **PASS with advice.**
   - `needs_another_mock` ➔ **HOLD:** Student must re-prepare and book a second mock session.
   - `not_ready` ➔ **FAIL:** Not recommended for this role profile.
6. Click **"Submit Scorecard"**.

### 6. Curating and Submitting Candidate Batches to Employers
Navigate to **Submissions** (`/placement-hr/submissions`).

   - Click **"Send Submission to Client"**.

### 11. Placement HR Best Practices & FAQs

- **Speed to Verify:** Strive to process new applications within **24 hours**. This keeps students engaged and accelerates batch creation.
- **Checklist Objectivity:** Always use the AI checklist as your anchor. Never mark a must-have skill as verified unless the candidate's CV clearly documents practical or project experience with that skill.
- **Constructive Rejections:** When rejecting an application, always provide specific, polite feedback so the candidate knows what skills to develop for future drives.

### 3. Submitting a Job Requisition
Navigate to **My Jobs** (`/client/jobs`).

1. Click **"+ Post New Requirement"** (`/client/jobs/new`).
2. Fill in your vacancy parameters:
   - **Role Title:** (e.g., *Business Analyst - Digital Banking*)

   - **Annual CTC Range:** Minimum and maximum package offered.
   - **Openings:** Number of vacancies.
   - **Job Description:** Paste your full JD, project scope, and key responsibilities.
3. Click **"Submit Requisition"**.
4. Our Placement HR team receives an immediate notification to create your AI checklist and start curating candidate batches.

### 4. Reviewing Curated Candidate Batches (Profiles, CVs & Mock Scores)
Navigate to **Submissions** (`/client/submissions`).

1. Whenever Placement HR delivers a new batch, you receive an email and in-app notification.
2. Click on the submission batch to open the **Candidate Showcase** (`/client/submissions/[id]`).
3. Each candidate card displays:
   - **Candidate Name, Location & Experience**
   - **Verified Match Score:** Alignment with your job requirements.
   - **Internal Mock Scorecard:** Ratings across Communication, Technical Knowledge, and Domain Acumen, along with the institute evaluator's notes.
   - **CV Viewer:** Click **"View Resume"** to inspect their complete PDF resume in a clean preview window without downloading.

### 5. Evaluating & Shortlisting Candidates
Navigate to **Candidates** (`/client/candidates/[id]`).

For every candidate in the batch, take action:
- **SHORTLIST FOR INTERVIEW:** Click the green **"Shortlist"** button. The candidate is immediately moved to your active interview pipeline.
- **REJECT CANDIDATE:** If a candidate does not meet your specific team fit, click **"Reject"**. Select a reason code from the dropdown (*Skill Mismatch*, *Experience Gap*, *Location Preference*, *Other*). This helps our placement team fine-tune future candidate batches for you.

---

### 6. Scheduling Interviews (Online Video Rounds & In-Person Visits)
Navigate to **Interviews** (`/client/interviews`).

For every shortlisted candidate:
1. Click **"Schedule Interview Round"** (`/client/interviews/[id]`).
2. Input the round details:
   - **Round Name:** Select or type the round name (*Round 1 — Technical Evaluation*, *Managerial Discussion*, *HR Discussion*, *Final Round*).
   - **Date & Start Time:** Select date and time.
   - **Duration:** Choose 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
   - **Interview Mode:**
     - **Online:** Paste your Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Zoom URL.
     - **Offline:** Select your office address where the candidate should report.
   - **Instructions for Candidate:** Add instructions (e.g., *"Please have your BRD project presentation ready on screen share"*).
3. Click **"Send Calendar Invite"**.
4. The system automatically sends calendar invites and email/in-app notifications to the candidate and Placement HR.

---

### 7. Submitting Post-Interview Feedback & Hiring Decisions
Navigate to the interview detail page post-session.

> **SLA Reminder:** Please submit feedback within **48 hours** of interview completion to keep candidates engaged and maintain recruitment momentum.

1. Locate the candidate card and click **"Submit Interview Feedback"**.
2. Fill out the quick feedback form:
   - **Rating:** 1 to 5 Stars on overall performance.
   - **Feedback Remarks:** Enter observations on technical depth, problem-solving, and communication.
3. **Select Official Decision:**
   - 🟢 **Selected:** Candidate cleared the round and is selected for hire! This unlocks the offer letter stage with Placement HR.
   - 🟡 **On Hold:** Candidate performed well but you want to compare with others before making a final decision.
   - 🔴 **Rejected:** Candidate was not selected for this opening.
4. Click **"Submit Feedback"**.

- View all candidates who have accepted your offers.
- Track their confirmed Date of Joining (DOJ).
- On the joining date, verify their arrival to complete the onboarding record.

# Cross-Role Process Summary: The Complete Journey

```
[Student Registration & Profile Setup]
  ➔ Student registers and completes profile to 80%+ (/student/profile)
  ➔ Admin approves student account (/admin/users/pending)
  ➔ Student uploads and parses primary resume (/student/cv)

[Job Posting & Checklist Approval]
  ➔ Client HR or Placement HR posts job vacancy (/client/jobs/new or /placement-hr/jobs/new)
  ➔ Placement HR generates AI Checklist & publishes job (/placement-hr/jobs/[id])
  ➔ Job appears on Student Job Feed with Match Score (/student/jobs)

[Application & Quality Gate]
  ➔ Student applies with 1-click (/student/jobs/[id])
  ➔ Placement HR claims and audits application against checklist (/placement-hr/applications/[id])
  ➔ Placement HR marks application as "Verified"
  ➔ Student books internal Mock Interview slot (/student/mocks)
  ➔ Evaluator conducts Mock and submits Scorecard with "Ready" recommendation (/placement-hr/mocks/[id])

[Client Batch Submission & Review]
  ➔ Placement HR creates Submission Batch with frozen snapshots (/placement-hr/submissions/new)
  ➔ Client HR reviews curated candidate showcase (/client/submissions/[id])
  ➔ Client HR shortlists candidate for interview (/client/candidates/[id])

[Interviewing & Selection]
  ➔ Client HR schedules Interview round with meeting link & instructions (/client/interviews/[id])
  ➔ Student attends interview
  ➔ Client HR submits 1-5 star feedback and selects candidate (/client/interviews/[id])

[Offer Letter & Official Placement]
  ➔ Placement HR uploads signed Offer Letter PDF and inputs terms (/placement-hr/offers/new)
  ➔ Student reviews CTC, downloads PDF, and clicks "Accept Offer" (/student/offers/[id])
  ➔ Placement HR confirms placement record (/placement-hr/placements)
  ➔ Student is officially PLACED! Celebratory status updated.
```
